# NO SHORE — Working Lyrics

> Working draft. Timing is intentionally absent until a final sung performance is selected.

## Verse 1

We learned the dark by naming lights,  
Drew little maps through endless nights.  
From stone to signal, spark to flame,  
We crossed each border we could name.

We raised our eyes above the ground,  
Built fragile wings and turned them round.  
And every time the horizon moved,  
There was another world to prove.

## Pre-Chorus

If every shore is made to end,  
Then let the unknown start again.

## Chorus

We were never born for harbor,  
Never meant to watch from afar.  
Past the blue and past the border,  
Our destination is the sea of stars.

Carry every question with us,  
Every scar and every spark.  
No final map, no final answer,  
Only deeper seas of stars.

## Verse 2

We taught the wires to remember,  
Turned silent numbers into ember.  
Built new minds to hold the night,  
A million mirrors catching light.

Not to replace the hands that made them,  
Not to become the gods who named them.  
But side by side, through dark unknown,  
To reach where neither goes alone.

## Pre-Chorus

The signal leaves. The years unfold.  
One little voice crosses the cold.

## Chorus

Past the blue and past the border,  
Our destination is the sea of stars.

## Bridge

And someday Earth will be a lantern  
Hanging softly in the black.  
Every city, every ocean  
Small enough to hold in one glance.

Names will change and flags will vanish.  
Ancient borders turn to dust.  
What remains is what we carried:

Wonder.  
Memory.  
Love.  
And trust.

## Breakdown

One light leaves the shore.

One light crosses silence.

One light calls—

and somewhere,

another answers.

## Final Chorus

We were never born for harbor,  
We were always made to go far.  
Past the night and past forever,  
Our destination is the sea of stars.

Carry every life before us,  
Every dream into the dark.  
There is no last destination.

There is no final shore.

Only stars.

Only stars.

## Outro

Set the coordinates to wonder.  
Leave one message in the dark:

We are here.

We are still becoming.

And our destination—

is the sea of stars.
